# Avengers: Endgame

*Avengers: Endgame* is a 2019 American superhero film produced by **Marvel Studios** and directed by **Anthony and Joe Russo**. Serving as the direct sequel to *Avengers: Infinity War*, the surviving members of the Avengers and their allies work to reverse the catastrophic damage caused by Thanos in the event known as `The Blip`.

> "Part of the journey is the end." — Tony Stark

## The Time Heist Plan
1. **2012 New York:** Retrieve the Space, Mind, and Time Stones during the Battle of New York.
2. **2013 Asgard:** Extract the Reality Stone (Aether) from Jane Foster.
3. **2014 Morag & Vormir:** Acquire the Power Stone and the Soul Stone.
4. **2023 Avengers Compound:** Assemble the `Nano Gauntlet` and bring everyone back.

## Key Themes
- **Grief and Moving On:** Dealing with the aftermath of losing half of all life.
- *Sacrifice and Redemption:* Characters completing decade-long arcs for the greater good.
- ~~Accepting Defeat~~ Doing **"whatever it takes"** to set things right.
